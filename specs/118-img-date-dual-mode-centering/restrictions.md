# Spec 118 — Restrictions

- Do not require users to recreate existing date widgets.
- Do not modify month or weekday behavior.
- Do not apply tabular cells to variable-length `TEXT_IMG`.
- Do not horizontally stretch glyphs.
- Do not persist generated data URLs in FVWF metadata.
- Do not share generated filenames across elements or MAIN/AOD.
- Do not advertise preview-only date effects as device-baked.
- Do not invent date-effect parameters in this spec.
- Do not touch Firebase Functions, rules, Storage, or Firestore.
- Do not deploy to the public remote.
- Do not include unrelated dirty entry files in specification or implementation commits.
